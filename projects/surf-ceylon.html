Surf Ceylon — AI Surf Tutor & Personalised Coaching Research

My contribution to a Sri Lankan surfing application focused on personalising surf training through user profiling, AI-assisted cardio recommendations, hybrid surfboard coaching, training guidance and progress tracking.

01 / Problem and objective

Surfers with different abilities need different conditioning, practice drills, board characteristics and suitable wave conditions. A one-size-fits-all training plan may not account for experience, goals, available equipment or limitations. My research workstream explored how a profile-driven coaching flow could make these recommendations more relevant and actionable.

02 / My individual contribution

  • AI Surf Tutor: independently developed the personal coaching workstream within the four-person capstone.
  • User journey: designed a seven-step profiling flow for collecting details useful to personalised recommendations.
  • Recommendation integration: contributed the connection between user-facing training screens, service requests and AI-generated suggestions.
  • Coaching and progress: worked within a training experience covering exercise plans, technique guidance and progress visibility.

03 / From user profile to recommendation

  1. Profile the surfer: capture height, weight, age, experience and skill level.
  2. Capture goals and constraints: ask about endurance, balance, paddling power, strength, available equipment and physical limitations.
  3. Prepare model inputs: transform profile responses into structured inputs suitable for recommendation services.
  4. Generate options: use the cardio recommendation and hybrid surfboard services to provide tailored options.
  5. Explain results: show exercises, rest/duration information, board properties, recommended wave height and coaching guidance.
  6. Track progress: keep a record of training activity and achievements over time.

These are six summarised process stages, not an assertion that the seven-step profile consists of exactly these screens.

04 / AI cardio plans

The research README describes a TensorFlow Neural Collaborative Filtering (NCF) model designed to recommend exercises by matching a surfer's characteristics and objectives to exercise options. The described output is three plan alternatives, each with 4–6 exercises and their duration and rest details.

05 / Hybrid surfboard and coaching recommendations

The AR Surfboard Coach combines rule/physics-based board length and volume calculations with a machine-learning prediction of indicative wave height. Inputs include surfer body measurements, age and experience; outputs include board recommendations, appropriate wave guidance and technique-oriented tips.

06 / Progress tracking and engagement

The wider training experience includes records of completed workouts, training minutes, streaks, technique practice and badges, with device-side persistence and MongoDB-backed data synchronisation described in the documentation. This helps users follow a longer learning journey rather than seeing a recommendation once and losing their history.

08 / Business analysis perspective

  • Requirements: captured the information needed to personalise training recommendations rather than imposing generic advice.
  • User journeys: structured input, evaluation, recommendation and follow-up as a coherent process.
  • Data and decision logic: linked profile attributes to the usefulness and explainability of recommendations.
  • Integration: considered how frontend forms, backend services, model responses and feedback need to align.
  • Evaluation: recognised that experimental model metrics are not equivalent to validated user benefits or safety improvements.

09 / Research evidence and limitations

This capstone connected a user-centred training experience with ML recommendation services. The project documentation describes performance results, but real-world effectiveness was not established by the materials reviewed. Future improvement areas include enlarging the surfboard training dataset, testing recommendations on broader user profiles and validating usefulness through structured user studies.
