# Inventory Management & Process Improvement

> **Document type:** Completed independent Business Analysis case study — simulated business scenario.
> **Prepared:** November 2026 · **Author:** Sabri Mazahim · **Version:** 1.0

A complete business-analysis documentation exercise for replacing delayed paper-to-spreadsheet stock updates with a controlled, role-based inventory process.

**Documentation package:** BRD, SRS, user stories and acceptance criteria, AS-IS/TO-BE process model, RTM and UAT test cases, risks and KPI definitions.

The business setting is fictional; deliverables are completed portfolio analysis artefacts, not claims of client implementation.
